Simple Step-by-Step Guide

How to use the Parent Portal

Follow these steps to create your parent account, add your child, make the school-fee payment, upload your payment proof and check your payment status later.

Important: Keep your username and password somewhere safe. You will need them to log back into the portal later to check your students, payments, verification status and receipts.
1

Create your parent account

Start by registering your personal parent account.

  1. Open the Rock Foundation Parent Portal.
  2. Click Create Parent Account or Create Account.
  3. Enter your full name and phone number.
  4. Enter your email address.
  5. Choose a username that you will remember.
  6. Create a password and enter it again to confirm it.
  7. Click Create Account.
Tip: Write down your username and password. Do not rely on memory alone.
2

Log in to your parent account

Use the same username and password whenever you return to the portal.

  1. Go to the Parent Login page.
  2. Enter your username.
  3. Enter your password.
  4. Click Log In.
3

Add your student

You must add the student before you can make a fee payment for that student.

  1. From your Parent Dashboard, click + Add Student.
  2. Enter the student's full name.
  3. Select the student's class.
  4. Select whether the student is an Old Student or New Intake.
  5. If the student is in SSS, select the correct department: Science or Art.
  6. Click Add Student.
Important: Make sure the student's name, class, student type and SSS department are correct before submitting.
4

Open the student's payment page

Your dashboard will show the student's current fee information.

  1. Find the student you want to pay for on your dashboard.
  2. Check the current session, term and school-fee amount shown.
  3. Click the student's Make Payment button.
The portal uses the student's class, student type and, where applicable, SSS department to determine the current fee.
5

Make the school-fee payment

Pay the full amount shown on the payment page using the school's official payment details.

  1. Read the school payment account details displayed on the payment page.
  2. Make the full payment into the official school account shown there.
  3. Confirm that the account details are correct before sending the money.
  4. Keep your payment confirmation or transaction evidence.
Do not use an old bank-account screenshot or an account number from another source. Always use the official payment details displayed by the portal at the time you are paying.
6

Upload your payment proof

After paying, return to the payment page and submit evidence of the payment.

  1. Make sure the payment amount matches the full school fee shown by the portal.
  2. Choose Payment Proof.
  3. Upload a clear screenshot, photograph or PDF showing the payment confirmation.
  4. Check that the uploaded file is readable before submitting.
  5. Click Submit Full Payment.
The portal accepts JPG, JPEG, PNG and PDF payment-proof files, with a maximum file size of 5 MB.
7

Check your payment status and receipt

Your payment is reviewed by the school after you submit it.

  1. After submission, your payment appears in your Parent Dashboard.
  2. A newly submitted payment will show as Pending until the school reviews it.
  3. When the school verifies it, the payment status changes to Verified.
  4. If there is a problem with the payment proof, the payment may be marked Rejected.
  5. When a receipt is available, use the receipt option shown with the payment.
You're done!
Once your student has been added and the payment has been submitted, you can leave the portal. Keep your login details safe and return later using the same username and password to check your student's payment records.
